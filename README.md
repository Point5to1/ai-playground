# AI Playground

**Part of [Point5to1™](https://github.com/point5to1)**

*Map the work. Redesign it for AI.*

AI Playground is a space for experimenting, designing, building, and evaluating practical AI-powered solutions.

Part of Point5to1™, it brings together experiments, agents, prototypes, skills, plugins, and calculators—sharing what's being built, tested, and learned along the way.

Projects range from early explorations to working prototypes. Not everything here is production-ready.

## How we work

**Our starting point is the work, not the technology.**

1. **Discover** — Understand the problem and the people doing the work.
2. **Map** — Capture activities, decisions, handoffs, and systems.
3. **Structure** — Organize context, rules, knowledge, and exceptions.
4. **Identify** — Determine where people, AI, and deterministic tools belong.
5. **Design** — Redesign the workflow and human-AI experience.
6. **Build** — Create an experiment, prototype, or working tool.
7. **Evaluate & Refine** — Test outcomes, learn, and improve.

Not every project follows every step. The aim is to learn quickly while keeping real-world use in view.

## Project documentation

Each project should have its own `README.md` explaining:

- **Problem:** What need or workflow does it address?
- **Approach:** What is being tested or built, and why?
- **Status:** Idea, exploring, prototype, evaluating, or archived
- **How to run:** Setup and usage, when applicable
- **What we learned:** Findings, limitations, and open questions
- **Next steps:** What should happen next?

Where useful, projects may include `WORKFLOW.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `KNOWLEDGE.md`, `EXCEPTIONS.md`, `EVALS.md`, or `DECISIONS.md`.

## From experiments to reusable knowledge

AI Playground captures what we're building and learning.

When an experiment produces useful, documented workflow knowledge, relevant assets may be curated into the [Workflow Library](https://github.com/point5to1/workflow-library).

Moving an asset there means it has been organized for reuse—not that a full production system has been delivered.

## Responsible experimentation

Use synthetic or properly authorized data. Never commit secrets, confidential customer information, or proprietary employer materials. Validate AI outputs before relying on them in consequential settings.
